## Decision

Do not pull `qwen3:8b` for this CPU-only host right now. `qwen3:4b` already runs at about 15-16 tok/s and has large reasoning overhead; `qwen3:8b` would likely be slower and less suitable as an OpenClaw fallback.

Practical recommendation:

- Keep `qwen3:4b` installed for experiments.
- Do not use it as the main local fallback yet.
- Prefer the already installed `qwen2.5:3b` for quick local fallback-style tasks until we test another non-reasoning model.
- Keep `nomic-embed-text` for local RAG retrieval.
