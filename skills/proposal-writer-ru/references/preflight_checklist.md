# Proposal Writer RU Preflight Checklist

Use before presenting substantial proposal drafts.

- Scope matches the user request.
- Draft is marked as internal/draft when appropriate.
- Exact prices and discounts are absent unless explicitly allowed in the current channel.
- Alpha licensing/SKU/tariff/tag/SLA/WEB/Terminal/RDP/Historian/Reports content is routed through alpha-licensing-qa or alpha-platform-presale.
- Product claims are source-backed or marked as assumptions.
- Customer-sensitive details are redacted before group sharing.
- Legal/compliance claims are not stated as advice, certification, validity, signature, acceptance, or enforceability.
- Exclusions and open questions are visible.
- No external send/publish/CRM write occurs without separate explicit approval.
